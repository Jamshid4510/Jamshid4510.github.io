Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FL Video Bot ("the app") handles information when you sign in with your Google account and authorize access to your YouTube channel.

Information we access

With your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to access your YouTube channel in order to upload videos and manage their titles, descriptions and related metadata. The app also receives OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google.

How we use information

Information is used only to perform the actions you request, such as uploading and updating videos on your own channel. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the device where the app runs and are not sent to any server operated by the app. Video files are sent directly to Google/YouTube.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles your data.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token file also removes the app's access on that device.
